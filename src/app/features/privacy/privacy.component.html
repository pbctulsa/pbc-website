01

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ church.name }} ("PBC," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in the PBC Songbook mobile app and related Songbook services.

02

Information we handle

When you browse songs

The app retrieves public song information from our hosted service. You do not need to provide personal information to browse or search the songbook. Our hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as an IP address, device or browser type, request time, and error or security logs, to deliver and protect the service.

Information stored on your device

A language preference may be saved locally so the service can remember your selection. This preference remains on your device until you change it, clear the app or browser data, or remove the app.

Information you choose to provide

If you submit a song correction or contact us for support, we may receive the content of your message, the proposed song changes, and any name or email address you choose to include. A name and email address are not required to browse the songbook.

What we do not request

PBC Songbook does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health information, or payment information.

03

How we use information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • provide, maintain, and improve the songbook;
  • remember your selected language on your device;
  • review song corrections and respond to messages when requested;
  • protect the service from abuse, fraud, and technical problems; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.
04

How information is shared

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information may be handled by service providers that help us host, secure, and operate the app, including Cloudflare. If you download the app through Apple, Apple separately handles App Store and device information under its own privacy terms.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of PBC, our users, or others.

05

Retention and your choices

Song correction submissions and support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to review the request, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Technical logs are retained according to our service providers' operational and security practices.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you submitted to us. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request, and some records may be retained where required by law. Use the contact information below to make a request.

06

Children's privacy

PBC Songbook is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

07

Security and third-party links

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

The app or related website may link to services we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, our practices, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above.
