Privacy Policy

Scripture stays with you. Your data does too.

PBC Thadou Kuki Bible works offline without an account. We do not collect your personal information, use analytics, show third-party ads, or track you across apps and websites.

Effective September 13, 2026

01

Offline by design

The complete Bible edition is bundled with the app; no account or download is needed.

02

No data collected

01

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ church.name }} ("PBC," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in the PBC Thadou Kuki Bible app for iPhone and iPad.

02

We do not collect personal data

PBC Thadou Kuki Bible does not send personal information or reading activity to PBC. The app has no user accounts, analytics tools, advertising networks, cloud synchronization, or app-operated server. Reading and searching the Bible does not require a network connection.

What the app does not request

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information, payment information, or advertising identifier.

03

Information stored on your device

The app saves your bookmarks, verse highlights, most recent reading position, text-size preference, and appearance preference locally on your device. PBC cannot access this information. It is used only to provide the features you choose and to restore your app experience when you return.

04

Sharing and external services

Sharing a verse

When you choose to share a verse, the app opens Apple's standard iOS sharing controls. You decide which person, app, or service receives the text. Any service you select handles that information under its own privacy policy; PBC does not receive a copy.

External links

The app contains optional links to PBC websites and Apple Books. These links open only when you select them. Apple, your browser, and the destination website may process standard connection information under their own privacy policies. Those services are not part of the Bible app's offline reading experience.

Apple may independently process App Store, device, crash, or diagnostic information based on your device and privacy settings. PBC does not control Apple's processing of that data.

05

Retention and your control

Because PBC does not collect or store your app data, we have no personal app data to retain, access, sell, or delete. You control the bookmarks, highlights, reading position, and preferences stored on your device. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data, subject to any device backups managed through your Apple account and settings.

06

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its Bible content and reading features work without an account or the submission of personal information.

07

Security

Keeping reading data on your device limits its exposure. Your device passcode, operating system security, backup settings, and the security of any service you choose through the iOS share sheet also affect the protection of information on your device.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app, our practices, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above. If a future app version begins collecting data, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details before that collection begins.
